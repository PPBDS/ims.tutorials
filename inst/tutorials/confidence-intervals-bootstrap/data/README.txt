medical_consultant.csv

The medical consultant case study from Chapter 12 of Introduction to Modern
Statistics (2e), "Confidence intervals with bootstrapping":
https://openintrostat.github.io/ims/foundations-bootstrapping.html
"her clients have had only 3 complications in the 62 liver donor surgeries she
has facilitated." The chapter gives only these counts, so the file has one row
per client (columns client, complication) with 3 "yes" and 59 "no". Which
clients are the three "yes" rows (14, 37, and 51) is our arbitrary choice.

tappers.csv

The tappers and listeners case study from the same chapter: in Elizabeth
Newton's study, "only 3 out of 120 listeners" guessed the tune. One row per
listener (columns listener, correct) with 3 "yes" and 117 "no". Which listeners
are the three "yes" rows (22, 68, and 105) is our arbitrary choice.
